Technikel Solutions — SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2025

We do not share your mobile opt-in data with third parties.

1) Scope

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how Technikel Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you participate in our SMS messaging program using our Microsoft Teams Phone numbers.

2) Information We Collect

  • Mobile phone number
  • Message content you send to us (only as needed to respond to your inquiry)
  • Technical and usage metadata (for example, timestamps, delivery status, and consent status)

3) How We Use Information

  • To deliver and support SMS communications you request
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To maintain records demonstrating consent, troubleshooting, and preventing misuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

4) Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your mobile opt-in data with third parties.

5) Data Retention

We retain SMS-related records for up to 24 months, or longer if required by law or to resolve disputes, after which we securely delete or anonymize them.

6) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information.

7) Your Choices

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance or to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact support@technikel.ca.

8) Children’s Privacy

Our SMS program isn’t directed to children under the age of 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

9) Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. We will reflect the effective date above.

10) Contact Us

If you have questions about this SMS Privacy Policy, contact support@technikel.ca.
